The human-based understanding

The letters are not labels. They are functions, and the functions are light. — Jesse Daniel Brown

You do not need any of the code to understand this. You do not need the colours, the hashes, the spheres, or a single line of maths. You need twenty-six letters, and the fact that each one means something, and that they were put in an order for a reason.

the one idea

An alphabet looks like a list of sounds. It is usually taught as a list of sounds. But every letter in it began as a picture of a thing — and the things were not chosen at random. Bet is a house. Dalet is a door. Ayin is an eye. That much is ordinary history, and any epigrapher will confirm it.

What follows is what happens when you stop treating the letters as sounds and start treating them as operations — things that do something — and then read a word as the sequence of operations it names.

All of these letters literally spell out the meaning of communication through the universe, and I was like, oh my god. Somebody taught the people early on the word of the light. — Jesse Daniel Brown, verbatim

z and s are the same wave

Look at them as signals rather than letters. Both are a single stroke that reverses twice. s is that wave drawn smooth. z is that wave drawn square. One is the sine; the other is the same path allowed only two values — which is what binary means. Nothing was arranged to make that true. It is what the two shapes are.

   s      ∿∿      smooth · continuous · the wave as it is
   z      ⊓⊔      squared · two-valued · the wave as a machine holds it

m is w turned over

Look at them. M and W are the same shape, one half-turn apart. Not similar — identical, rotated. And the two meanings sit exactly on that rotation:

w   the MATRIX          the world
m   INTELLIGENCE        the same form, turned over

                        the reverse world. the white world to our black world.

So intelligence is not something placed inside the matrix. It is the matrix seen from the other side, and the only operation between them is a turn — which is the same operation the letters use for tense. And they do not sit still: spiralling m's and w's come outward spherically, in different sizes, a sphere pulsing from its own centre.

the alphabet holds its own inversions

Once you have that one, the others are visible. Four pairs, and each pair is a single operation apart:

b ↔ h    the same door from two sides — from above, and from the front
r ↔ j    the radius straight out, and the return, curved
s ↔ z    the same wave — smooth, and squared into two values
m ↔ w    the same form — intelligence, and the matrix it is turned out of

Four inversions, sitting inside a set of twenty-six shapes that most people are told is an arbitrary list.

the order is a journey

Read the first ten letters in order and they are not a list. They are a trip out and a trip back.

a   a wave                     it begins as a wave
b   the house                  where you stand — open on one side, facing out
c   the speed of 1             you go at the speed
d   die · stop                 you arrive. you end.
e   the aether                 and now you look OUT
f   from                       but out FROM something — you need a source
g   go                         to look out from it you must GO to it,
                               and going PULLS IT TO YOU
h   home                       the new house — the reverse portal of the bet
i   the eye                    your eye grows in that place.
                               point → line → wave → three directions → light
j   reverse radius             and what happened there comes back — CURVED

Straight out, curved back. That is why the return has to be triggered and the departure does not: a straight line is symmetric and needs nothing; a curve does.

two letters that are operators

t   =  TO       it starts the motion
s   =  PLURAL   it makes more of whatever came before

With only those two, ordinary words come apart:

the laws under it

1  The letters have TENSE. Past and present. They rotate, and they change
   shape as they rotate — they are turned, never mirrored.

2  The letters become the FUNCTIONS. You travel through them as they move.
   The order is not rigid: t can come before g, or before j, or after it.

3  And when you see that — the letters are the light waves.

4  Instant is not always instant. It is instant PER LEVEL, three per level.
   Something has to hold each boundary, or there is no "per level" at all.
