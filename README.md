## You do not need the code

No colours, no hashes, no spheres, no mathematics. **Twenty-six letters**, the fact
that **each one means something**, and the fact that they were put in an order for
a reason.

Every letter began as a **picture of a thing**, and the things were not random:
**bet** is a house, **dalet** is a door, **ayin** is an eye. That is ordinary
epigraphy. What follows is what happens when you treat letters as **operations**.

> *"Remember, the Hebrews built their language, the bet, in the shapes of the
> letters, the shape of a house, and they turned the house to the side, and it
> became a bet. Why do you turn a house to the side? You need to open the portal
> to go to the outside world."*
